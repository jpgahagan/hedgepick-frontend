02 How it works

Twelve agents. Five stages. One ruling.

Each analyst sees only its own data. The researchers argue from those reports, and nothing reaches the portfolio manager until the risk desk has argued over it. Scroll to follow a report through the pipeline.

Stage 01

Evidence

Four analysts, working separately

Four analysts each check one thing: the price chart, what investors are saying, the news, and the company’s finances. None of them sees the others’ work.

Market: trend vs 50/200-day averages, MACD, RSI, Bollinger, volume. Sentiment: social and retail positioning. News: company events, guidance, macro and sector catalysts. Fundamentals: revenue and EPS trend, margins, FCF, leverage, valuation.

Stage 02

Bull vs bear

Two researchers, one debate

One researcher argues to buy and one argues against. Each has to answer the other’s points using the analysts’ evidence and your own reasoning.

Adversarial rebuttal between bull and bear researchers, built on the four analyst reports and the user’s thesis. Each side must address the other’s strongest claim.

Stage 03

Research manager

Judges the debate

A research manager reads both sides, decides which case was stronger, and writes an investment plan.

Scores the debate on evidence rather than rhetoric, states which arguments carried, and produces a written investment plan for the trader.

Stage 04

Trader and risk desk

One trader, three risk analysts

A trader turns the plan into a position. Then aggressive, neutral and conservative risk analysts argue about how much to buy and where to get out.

Trader proposal with entry, stop loss and take profit. Three-way risk debate on position size relative to volatility, drawdown and event risk.

Stage 05

Portfolio manager

The final call

The portfolio manager weighs everything and makes the call: Buy, Hold or Sell, with the reasons in writing.

Final BUY / HOLD / SELL rating with written rationale, position sizing and risk levels. Every ruling is published as-is, including the SELLs.

Tell it why you want to buy. It tries to prove you wrong.
