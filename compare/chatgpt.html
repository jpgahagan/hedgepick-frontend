HedgePick vs asking ChatGPT

ChatGPT answers the question you ask. HedgePick runs a fixed process designed to argue against you.

Choose ChatGPT if

  • You want a fast conversational answer or explanation of a concept.
  • You want to explore many unrelated questions in one place.
  • You’re comfortable writing careful prompts and checking sources yourself.

Choose HedgePick if

  • You want the same rigorous process every time, not a prompt you have to get right.
  • You want a dedicated bear whose only job is to find what’s wrong with the trade.
  • You want a ruling, trade levels and a PDF you can keep.
