04 Questions

Before you share.

Do I need to be approved?
No. Your link works the moment you create it. We review partners and may remove anyone using spam, paid search on our brand name, or misleading claims.
Can I try HedgePick before recommending it?
Yes. Everyone gets three free analyses. If you have an audience, reply to your welcome email and we'll set up complimentary access so you can review the full reports.
What counts as a referral?
Any purchase made within 90 days of someone clicking your link, on the same browser. Last partner link clicked gets the credit.
What can't I say?
Don't promise returns, accuracy, or that HedgePick picks winners. It's research software that argues both sides. Not financial advice.
