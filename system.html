Methodology How a ruling is made

Twelve agents. Built to disagree.

Most AI tools answer the question you asked, the way you asked it. HedgePick splits the work across twelve agents with different jobs, makes two of them argue opposite sides, and makes a risk desk argue again before anyone rules. This page shows every step, where the data comes from, and what we have not proven yet.

01 The pipeline

Follow one report from data to ruling.

The diagram tracks the stage you are reading. Every report, including the free ones, runs the full pipeline.

Stage 01 · 4 agents

Evidence

Each analyst writes its own report from its own data. None of them sees the others’ work.

  • Market analystPrice history and indicators: 50/200-day averages, MACD, RSI, Bollinger bands, volume.
  • Sentiment analystWhat retail investors are saying on StockTwits and Reddit.
  • News analystCompany news, guidance and macro and sector headlines.
  • Fundamentals analystIncome statement, balance sheet, cash flow and valuation.
Stage 02 · 2 agents

Bull vs bear

Two researchers argue opposite sides from the same four reports and your thesis, and each has to answer the other.

  • Bull researcherBuilds the strongest case for owning the stock.
  • Bear researcherBuilds the strongest case against it, and attacks your stated reasoning first.
Stage 03 · 1 agent

Research manager

Reads the debate, decides which side carried it on the evidence, and writes an investment plan.

  • Research managerJudges the debate and hands a written plan to the trader.
Stage 04 · 4 agents

Trader and risk desk

The plan becomes a concrete position, then three risk analysts argue about it from different temperaments.

  • TraderProposes entry, stop loss, take profit and size.
  • Aggressive analystArgues for more exposure when the upside justifies it.
  • Neutral analystWeighs both and argues for balance.
  • Conservative analystArgues for protecting capital and tighter risk.
Stage 05 · 1 agent

Portfolio manager

Weighs the full record and issues the final rating with its reasons. That ruling is what you see on page one.

  • Portfolio managerFinal BUY, HOLD or SELL, with rationale, risk levels and sizing.
02 Data

Where the evidence comes from.

Agents pull current data at the time of each analysis. Every report records the date and price it was run at.

Prices

Yahoo Finance

Daily price and volume history for the stock, used by the market analyst and the screener.

Indicators

Computed in-house

Moving averages, MACD, RSI and Bollinger bands are calculated from that price history, not copied from a third party.

Financials

Yahoo Finance financials

Income statement, balance sheet, cash flow, estimates and valuation ratios.

News

Yahoo Finance news

Company headlines plus a fixed set of macro topics: rates, central banks, energy and supply chains.

Sentiment

StockTwits and Reddit

Recent posts about the ticker, read for tone and positioning. Treated as a crowd signal, not as evidence.

Reasoning

Anthropic Claude

The agents are large language models from Anthropic, each given a different role, its own data and its own instructions.

03 The daily screener

How the public debates are chosen.

Every weekday morning a screener scans liquid US-listed stocks for converging technical and fundamental signals. The highest scorers go through the full pipeline and are published at /analysis, whatever the ruling.

// each signal returns a strength from 0 to 1
strength = 0.4 + 0.6 × how hard it fired
// weighted, then scaled to 0–100
score = 100 × Σ(weight × strength) ÷ Σ(weights)
  • Universe. US-listed stocks priced at $10 or more, with at least 100,000 shares and $5 million traded a day on average.
  • Threshold. A stock needs a score of 8 or more to be considered. The top 10 are kept.
  • Weights are judgement calls. They reflect how much we trust each signal. They have not been fitted to past returns.

04 What we haven't proven

We don't publish an accuracy number.

Because we haven't measured one we can stand behind. HedgePick's value is the argument it shows you, not a hit rate.

Our standard

Earlier versions of this site showed percentages that weren't backed by a rigorous test. We removed them.

If we publish a track record, it will be dated, complete, misses included, measured against the S&P 500, and linked from this page.

Language models can misread data or state things confidently that are wrong. Read the debate, check the numbers that matter to you, and treat every ruling as research, not a recommendation.
